A little more about getting started with MaiChat.

What is MaiChat?

MaiChat is an open source AI roleplay app for Android, built with Flutter. It brings conversations, characters, lorebooks, scenarios, and creative tools together in one customizable app.

Do I need my own AI provider?

Yes. MaiChat connects to providers you configure. Add your preferred provider and API key or endpoint in the app. Provider pricing, availability, and account requirements vary.

Can I bring my existing characters?

MaiChat can import compatible character cards and content from SillyTavern, Agnai, and Risu. Support depends on the file format and content, so keep a copy of your originals.

How do I install it on Android?

Download the APK from our download page, open the file on your Android device, and follow the system's installation prompts. That page also has step by step instructions.

Is the app free?

MaiChat is free and open source. AI requests use your configured provider, which may charge separately according to its own pricing.
