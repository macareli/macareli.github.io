Make yourself at home.

You’ll need an Android device and an AI provider of your choice. Install MaiChat, then add your provider in the app settings.

How to install
  1. Tap Download APK on your Android device.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your browser or Files app.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install this app, then follow the installation prompts.
  4. Open MaiChat and configure your AI provider to start chatting.
Updating an existing installation?

Open the APK and follow Android’s update prompt. You can export a backup from MaiChat before updating. Android can update an existing app when the package and signing key match.

Downloaded on your computer?

Transfer the APK to your Android device, then open it with the Files app. MaiChat’s APK installs on Android; it does not run directly on your computer.
